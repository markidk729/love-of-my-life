LOVE OF MY LIFE

Some feelings are too beautiful to leave unsaid.

My Love ❤️

Sometimes, I get scared that someday you will leave me. Not because I don't trust you, but because a part of me is always afraid that one day, loving me will become too much for you. That maybe I'll become a burden—someone whose problems, emotions, and presence slowly become too heavy to carry.

I fear the day when you might look at me differently, when talking to me starts feeling like a responsibility instead of something you genuinely want to do. I fear becoming someone you stay with out of guilt rather than love.

And maybe that's why sometimes I overthink the smallest changes. A shorter reply, a different tone, a little distance. I start wondering if you're getting tired of me, if I'm asking for too much, or if one day you'll realize that life would be easier without me in it.

But at the same time, the thought of losing you scares me more than I know how to explain.

So if there ever comes a time when things feel too heavy, please don't just disappear. Talk to me. Let me understand what you're feeling.

Because more than anything, I think I'm just scared of waking up one day and realizing that the person I thought would stay had quietly decided that I was too much to keep.

And honestly, I hope that day never comes.

Forever hoping it's us. ❤️
